# Catálogo de técnicas CapCut → Remotion (17 projetos "EditAssets")

> **Resumo (pt-BR):** catálogo de técnicas de texto, elementos, máscaras e looks extraídas de 17 projetos CapCut de referência ("EditAssets"), com os valores de keyframe convertidos para Remotion, a sugestão de SFX e uma nota de 1 a 3 estrelas. O corpo está em inglês (termos técnicos). A coluna "Identity" se refere à identidade de exemplo *grace* (Reels católicos: preto/branco/#5B4BFF, editorial, sem contorno) — adapte à sua.

**Built from** `analysis.md` (Ferramentas in sections 1-5, the other 16 projects in section 6) and the per-project extracts in `_extract/projects/<Name>/`.
- The exact numbers are CapCut keyframe values, in canvas **half-units**: x/y from −1 to +1 across the frame, y up. Scale is a multiplier and time is seconds.
- **CapCut → Remotion conversion:**
  - px_x = x · 540, px_y = −y · 960
  - 1 CapCut "8-frame step" = 0.267 s at 30 fps
  - The curve names map to cubic beziers:
    - CurveIn ≈ `Easing.bezier(0.42,0,1,1)`
    - CurveOut ≈ `(0,0,0.58,1)`
    - CurveInOut ≈ `(0.42,0,0.58,1)`
    - FreeCurve = its own handles
- **Identity column:** how to use each technique inside the example *grace* identity (v11) (black / white / blue-violet `#5B4BFF`, editorial premium, **no outlines/strokes**, Catholic reels).
- **SFX column:** a category from `sfx/SFX_LICENCAS.md`. Missing pop / bubble / pen / bell sounds come from the CC0 set in `sfx/cc0/`.
- **Rating:** ★★★ = recommended, ★★ = use occasionally, ★ = only with a strong reason.

## Recomendações principais (identidade de exemplo *grace*)
1. **Word rise + two-weight serif/sans lockup:** A1 + A2 + A3, with a **1-frame `#5B4BFF` accent flash** on the key word (A13).
2. **Stacked tonal echo** (A6) and **giant ghost serif word** (A15) for chapter/thesis words.
3. **Staggered pills** (B2) for lists (the 3 conditions, the sacraments), each with a quiet pop.
4. **Counters / date tickers** (B4 / B5) with tabular numerals and a clock tick → ding.
5. **Crosshair registration lines** (B8), plus **thin orbit rings** (B6) around a sacred icon.
6. **Split-mask wipes** (C1) and **blur pulse on cuts** (C6) as the transitions, with a short bright whoosh.
7. **1-frame stutter** (C7) at most once, on the punch word.
8. **Printed B&W + FPS-lag** (D2) only on sacred-art inserts, matching the Perfeccionismo look.
